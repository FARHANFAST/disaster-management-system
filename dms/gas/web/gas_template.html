Evacuating around an H₂S plume

A pipe leak at tank H-4 sends H₂S over building B1. Outdoors the gas is a sum of Gaussian puffs that drift with the wind, so the plume bends when the wind turns. Each room and corridor segment is a well-mixed box that slowly takes in outdoor air. When a facade detector reads 30 ppm for 5 s the alarm latches, the HVAC shuts down and people head out. Every time they reach a node of the navigation graph, they re-plan with A*, avoiding any node that will be at or above 10 ppm when they get there (according to the 5-minute forecast). An exit's level is the worst air on the outdoor walk from that door to its assembly point. Switch to Gas-blind to watch the same people take the plain fastest route.
